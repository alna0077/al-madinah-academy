01

Qur’an Recitation

Careful correction from first sounds to fluent recitation.

For non-Arabic speakers and learners who want stronger pronunciation, accuracy, fluency, and Tajweed foundations.

Students learn Makharij, similar sounds, harakat, sukoon, madd, tanween, shaddah, stopping, short surahs, and gradual Tajweed foundations.

Learning approach Guided recitation, patient correction, repetition, review, and progression at the student’s actual level.

Advanced direction Memorization and Qira’at may form part of an appropriate learning path according to level and teacher assessment.

02

Arabic Language

Arabic with purpose.

A major academy pathway for children and adults who want to read, write, speak, and understand Arabic step by step.

Foundations Letters, sounds, joining letters, reading fluency, writing practice, and vocabulary.

Progression Students move from words to sentences and build confidence with Qur’anic and Islamic vocabulary.

Purpose Language goals remain connected to Qur’an recitation, comprehension, and Islamic learning materials.

See Arabic outcomes

Little Qur’an Learners

Gentle structure for young learners.

A child-focused program combining Arabic letters, short surahs, repetition, memorization support, and Islamic manners.

Children learn with encouragement, clear classroom expectations, age-appropriate practice, and practical communication with parents. The pace supports confidence without losing the importance of correction and consistency.

Islamic Studies

Sound foundations connected to daily practice.

Essential learning in Tawheed, belief, worship, manners, and daily conduct, taught with clarity and appropriate pacing.

The program gives students an organized foundation rather than disconnected information. Lessons emphasize understanding, reliable teaching, review, and applying knowledge through worship and conduct.

Format: Online and in-person options depend on level, program demand, and teacher availability.
