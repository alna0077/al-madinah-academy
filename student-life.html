Student Life

A calm place for attentive learning.

The academy environment is prepared for focused Qur’an, Arabic, and Islamic Studies learning—with order, respect, and care for each student’s next step.

Learning atmosphere

Calm and respectful, with a clear purpose.

A settled environment helps students listen carefully, practise without embarrassment, and receive correction with confidence.

Classes bring together instruction, recitation, discussion, writing, repetition, and review. Adab shapes how students enter the learning space, engage with teachers and classmates, and carry knowledge into daily conduct.

Inside a lesson

Correction becomes a path forward.

Students see, hear, repeat, and apply new material in manageable steps.

  1. Settle

    Begin with focus, respect, and a clear expectation.

  2. Review

    Return to prior material before adding something new.

  3. Learn

    Study a sound, rule, word, or concept with guidance.

  4. Practise

    Recite, read, write, answer, and receive correction.

  5. Anchor

    Leave with a specific next step for continued progress.

Learning materials

Tools chosen for the stage of learning.

Qur’an passages, Arabic letters, books, puzzles, and written examples each serve a specific teaching purpose.

Young learners may work with tactile Arabic materials and repetition. Developing readers move through sounds, joined letters, words, and sentences. Qur’an students use visible examples and direct teacher feedback to strengthen accuracy and fluency.

For students & families

A learning path families can understand.

Parents stay connected to the student’s starting point, goals, and next steps through practical communication.

  • Placement reflects the student’s current reading, recitation, or Arabic level.
  • Children receive age-appropriate expectations and gentle accountability.
  • Private support may be considered when a student needs a more focused plan.
  • Online learning is available alongside the academy’s Ottawa in-person environment.

Ottawa & online

A real classroom, with learning that can continue beyond it.

The in-person setting gives students a grounded, family-oriented academy experience in East Ottawa. Online options extend access while keeping the same emphasis on preparation, attentive teaching, review, and adab.
