About Al-Madinah

Learning rooted in the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah.

Al-Madinah is an Ottawa academy for children, families, sisters, and adult learners seeking structured Qur’an, Arabic, and Islamic Studies education in a calm, respectful environment.

Mission & approach

Strong foundations, taught with clarity and care.

Our aim is to connect students to the Qur’an and Sunnah through careful correction, clear teaching, and knowledge that informs practice.

Qur’an study develops correct recitation, Tajweed, and steady confidence. Arabic opens a pathway toward reading and understanding. Islamic Studies builds sound foundations in belief, worship, manners, and daily conduct. Across every subject, students progress through assessment, guided practice, review, and patient feedback.

Adab is part of the learning itself: students are encouraged to listen attentively, ask respectfully, practise consistently, and carry what they learn into everyday life.

Educational framework

Qur’an · Sunnah · Knowledge · Adab

Correct with patience. Accuracy grows through attentive teaching and repeatable practice.

Progress with structure. Each next step is grounded in what the student can already do.

Connect knowledge to practice. Learning should shape worship, character, and conduct.

Leadership & educators

Teaching leadership and academy direction, working together.

Al-Madinah’s educational work is guided by two distinct, complementary areas of responsibility.

Ustadh Emad guides the academy’s vision, structure, operations, educational systems, and community work. Ustadhah Ayah leads Qur’an and Islamic Studies teaching with deep experience in recitation, Qira’at, memorization, and patient correction.

Co-Founder, Academy Director & Islamic Studies Educator

Ustadh Emad

More than two decades of professional, educational, and community experience guiding the academy’s vision and development.

Background & Studies

A graduate of King Saud University in Riyadh, he spent formative years living and studying in an Arabic-speaking environment shaped by Islamic learning and culture. Alongside his academic and professional background, he has continued studies in Aqeedah, Tawheed, Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, Tajweed, Arabic, Nahw, and classical Islamic texts, including al-Usool ath-Thalathah, al-Arba‘un an-Nasawiyyah, ‘Umdat al-Ahkam, and al-Muwatta’. He has received ijazat from qualified shuyukh in a number of these works and areas of study.

Qur’an & Teaching

His connection with the Qur’an began from an early age and has continued through study, teaching, and community involvement. As a native Arabic speaker fluent in English, he supports both Arabic-speaking and non-Arabic-speaking learners with clarity.

Community Work in Ottawa

In Ottawa, his community work has included developing Qur’an halaqas and Islamic educational programs for children, youth, and adults; initiating new courses and programs; curriculum development and selection; class organization; family communication; instructor coordination; and service as a local masjid board member. He has also helped introduce community initiatives, including a summer camp program for children.

Academy Direction

His professional background in project management, technology, and digital systems supports the Academy’s structure, educational platforms, program delivery, operations, and long-term initiatives.

Educational Approach

At Al-Madinah Quran & Sunnah Academy, his teaching and educational direction are rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah upon the understanding of as-Salaf as-Salih. Working alongside Ustadhah Ayah, he helps guide the Academy’s educational vision with a focus on clear, trustworthy instruction that connects foundational Islamic knowledge and Arabic understanding with everyday life.

Co-Founder & Lead Qur’an & Islamic Studies Educator

Ustadhah Ayah

More than 25 years of Qur’an education with deep experience in recitation, Qira’at, memorization, and Islamic Studies.

Qur’an & Qira’at

She has received ijazat in Qira’at, including Hafs and Shu‘bah from ‘Aasim and Qalun from Nafi‘. She actively teaches these recitations and grants ijazat to qualified students upon successful completion of the required recitation and study.

Islamic Studies

Alongside her Qur’anic studies, Ustadhah Ayah has pursued studies in Aqeedah, Tawheed, Fiqh, Hadith, and classical Islamic texts, including al-Usool ath-Thalathah, al-Arba‘un an-Nasawiyyah, ‘Umdat al-Ahkam as-Sughra, ‘Umdat al-Ahkam al-Kubra, and al-Muwatta’. She has received ijazat from qualified shuyukh in a number of these works and areas of study.

Teaching Approach

Throughout her teaching, she has worked with students of different ages and levels, with particular emphasis on correct recitation, Tajweed, memorization, patient correction, and steady progress. As a native Arabic speaker fluent in English, she supports both Arabic-speaking and non-Arabic-speaking learners with clarity, patience, and care, helping students build confidence, adab, and a stronger connection to the Qur’an.

Program Leadership in Ottawa

In Ottawa, she has served as a Qur’an program coordinator at local masajid, developing and introducing new classes and overseeing a girls’ program serving approximately 80–100 students. Her responsibilities included program planning, curriculum development and selection, class organization, student and family communication, and coordination and supervision of teaching staff.

Her additional studies in administration, digital design, and graphic design also contribute to the Academy’s organization, educational resources, and learning environment.

Academy Role

At Al-Madinah Quran & Sunnah Academy, Ustadhah Ayah helps lead the Qur’an and Islamic Studies programs, with teaching rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah upon the understanding of as-Salaf as-Salih. Working alongside Ustadh Emad, she helps guide the Academy’s educational direction with a focus on authentic knowledge, careful teaching, and helping learners strengthen their relationship with Allah through sound Islamic learning.

Sisters’ learning initiative

Rooted in Ottawa. Teaching beyond borders.

Led by Ustadhah Ayah, this dedicated initiative supports sisters and girls in a calm, comfortable Islamic learning environment.

Teaching may include Qur’an, Qira’at where appropriate, Arabic support, and Islamic Studies. Alongside learners in Ottawa, online study currently reaches students in Halifax and the United States—modest evidence that careful teaching can remain personal across distance.

Online participation is arranged according to student level, program fit, and teacher availability.
