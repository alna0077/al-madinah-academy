The Academy

About Al-Madinah Academy.

Structured Qur’an, Arabic, Tajweed, and Islamic Studies education for children, youth, and adults—in East Ottawa and online.

Who we are

A family-focused academy with a clear educational purpose.

Al-Madinah serves families in Orléans and East Ottawa, while online learning makes the same careful instruction available beyond the local classroom.

Students study Qur’an recitation, Tajweed, Arabic, and Islamic Studies through structured lessons, guided practice, correction, and review. Each subject has its own purpose, while knowledge and adab connect the academy experience as a whole.

We welcome children beginning with Arabic letters, youth building stronger foundations, adults returning to recitation, and non-Arabic speakers seeking clearer access to Qur’anic language and Islamic learning.

Leadership & educators

Teaching leadership and academy direction, working together.

Two complementary areas of responsibility guide Al-Madinah: academy structure and community direction, alongside deep Qur’an and Islamic Studies teaching experience.

Co-Founder, Academy Director & Islamic Studies Educator

Ustadh Emad

More than two decades of professional, educational, and community experience guide his work in academy direction, teaching, and program development.

A graduate of King Saud University in Riyadh, Ustadh Emad has continued studies in Aqeedah, Tawheed, Fiqh, Hadith, Seerah, Tajweed, Arabic, Nahw, and foundational texts including al-Usool ath-Thalathah, al-Arba‘un an-Nasawiyyah, ‘Umdat al-Ahkam, and al-Muwatta’. He has received ijazat from qualified shuyukh in a number of these works and areas of study.

His work brings together Arabic and Tajweed teaching, community Qur’an and Islamic education, technology and systems, and long-term academy operations. In Ottawa, that experience has included program and curriculum development, instructor and family coordination, children’s and adult learning initiatives, and service as a local masjid board member.

Co-Founder & Lead Qur’an & Islamic Studies Educator

Ustadhah Ayah

More than 25 years of Qur’an education with deep experience in recitation, Qira’at, memorization, and Islamic Studies.

Ustadhah Ayah memorized the Qur’an from an early age and completed teacher-preparation studies at Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University. Her ijazat in recognized Qira’at include Hafs and Shu‘bah from ‘Aasim and Qalun from Nafi‘; she teaches these recitations and grants ijazah to qualified students after the required recitation and study.

Her studies also include Aqeedah, Tawheed, Fiqh, Hadith, and recognized foundational works, with ijazat from qualified shuyukh in a number of texts and areas of study. A native Arabic speaker fluent in English, she has extensive experience teaching learners at different levels and coordinating Qur’an programs with patient correction, careful review, and attention to adab.

Sisters’ learning initiative

Rooted in Ottawa. Teaching beyond borders.

Led by Ustadhah Ayah, this dedicated initiative supports sisters and girls in a calm, comfortable Islamic learning environment. Alongside learners in Ottawa, online study currently reaches students in Halifax and the United States.

Local & online

In-person in East Ottawa. Accessible online.

In-person learning in Orléans gives children, youth, adults, sisters, and families a grounded academy environment. Online options extend access to Arabic-speaking and non-Arabic-speaking learners while preserving the same emphasis on preparation, attentive instruction, review, and adab.
